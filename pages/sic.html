Where Nature, Heritage & Serenity Converge

Siwa Oasis is an enchanting sanctuary nestled in Egypt's Western Desert. From crystal-clear hypersaline lakes and natural hot springs to ancient mud-brick fortresses and dramatic golden dunes, Siwa offers an experience unlike anywhere else on Earth.

At Siwa Information Center (SIC), powered by Totos Group, we provide in-depth travel guidance, customized itineraries, licensed 4x4 desert permits, and direct connections to trusted local guides.

Best Time to Visit & Desert Packing Guide

Siwa Oasis offers distinct seasonal wonders throughout the year.
Here is everything you need to know about climate, seasonal highlights, and what to pack.

Autumn (Oct - Nov)

Prime Season

24°C - 28°C | Mild & Sunny

The most popular time for visitors. Olive and date harvesting festivals, crisp evenings, and ideal conditions for desert safaris and salt lake swimming.

Winter (Dec - Feb)

Stargazing & Springs

18°C - 22°C | Crisp Nights

Warm sunny afternoons with cool desert nights (8°C–12°C). Perfect for soaking in hot sulfur springs, campfire Bedouin dinners, and astronomical photography.

Spring (Mar - May)

Blooming Oasis

26°C - 32°C | Pleasant Breezes

Lush green date palm groves, blooming desert flora, and tranquil atmosphere before summer heat begins. Excellent for heritage touring and sandboarding.

Summer (Jun - Aug)

Healing Sand Baths

36°C - 42°C | Dry Desert Heat

Famous for the therapeutic hot sand baths of Mount Dakrour, treating rheumatism and joint ailments under expert Siwan medical healers.

What to Pack for Siwa

The Western Desert experiences wide day-to-night temperature variations. Packing the right essentials ensures maximum comfort and enjoyment.

Ask Siwa Info Desk
Swimwear & Slippers

Swimwear for hypersaline salt lakes (brings high buoyancy!) and sandals for rocky shores.

Sun & Sand Protection

Polarized UV sunglasses, high-SPF sunscreen, wide-brim hat, and scarf for dune wind.

Layered Clothing

Lightweight cotton clothing for day; warm fleece jacket or hoodie for chilly desert evenings.

Cash in Egyptian Pounds

ATM availability in the oasis can be limited; carry cash for local artisan shops, dates, and tips.
