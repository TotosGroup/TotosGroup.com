Authentic Eco-Hospitality

A Peaceful Oasis Retreat Built with Traditional Kersheef

Totos Lodge invites you to slow down and experience the soul of Siwa Oasis. Handcrafted using traditional Kersheef (ancient natural salt-rock and clay mortar) and sustainable palm wood, our lodge harmonizes seamlessly with the stunning desert surroundings.

Surrounded by swaying date palms and tranquil olive groves, Totos Lodge combines authentic Berber aesthetics with modern comfort. Enjoy farm-to-table organic dining, mineral-rich salt spring relaxation, and stargazing evenings around a crackling Bedouin fire.

A message from our hospitality team:

"We envisioned Totos Lodge as a sanctuary where travelers can unplug from modern noise and immerse themselves in the healing peace, warm hospitality, and timeless beauty of Siwa."

Why Choose Totos Lodge

Natural Thermal Living

Our thick Kersheef salt-rock walls provide natural thermal regulation—keeping your room refreshingly cool under the desert sun and pleasantly warm during crisp desert nights.

Farm-to-Table Cuisine

Every meal is prepared daily using locally harvested Siwan olives, virgin cold-pressed oils, organic dates, fresh vegetables, and traditional slow-cooked Bedouin recipes.

Wellness & Immersion

Rejuvenate in private thermal hot springs, float in crystal salt pools, participate in outdoor yoga sessions, and embark on custom tours with Siwa Guide right from our doorstep.
