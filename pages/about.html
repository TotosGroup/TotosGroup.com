About Us

Home / About Us

Empowering Businesses, Inspiring Journeys

At Totos Group, we specialize in delivering integrated solutions for lifestyle, travel, and retail. Our diverse services—from e-commerce and grocery distribution on Amazon to destination centers in Alexandria and Siwa, comfortable lodging, and on-demand transportation—are designed to empower businesses, enrich experiences, and drive success.

Rooted in innovation and guided by an unwavering commitment to excellence, we strive to connect people, simplify daily living, and make a lasting positive impact across communities.

Get In Touch With Us

Our Mission

Empowering businesses and enriching lives through innovative, convenient solutions and a dedication to exceptional service and reliability across every customer touchpoint.

Our Vision

To be a regional benchmark in integrated lifestyle and travel ecosystems, seamlessly connecting commerce, culture, and mobility across Egypt and beyond.

Our History

Founded by Mohamed Othman, Totos Group grew from an ambitious vision into a trusted multi-sector enterprise uniting top-rated retail, destination guidance, and hospitality.

Our Values

Integrity, client obsession, and continuous innovation guide every decision, building lasting trust and meaningful relationships with our partners and clients.

Our Services

Encompassing Amazon e-commerce retail, Alexandria & Siwa tourism information hubs, Totos Lodge eco-retreats, and Totos CarGo transportation.

Our Commitment

Dedicated to delivering solutions that inspire growth, enhance daily living, and create unforgettable travel memories backed by professionalism and authenticity.
