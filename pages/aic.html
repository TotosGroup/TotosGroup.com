Best Time to Visit Alexandria & City Guide

Alexandria boasts a refreshing Mediterranean climate with vibrant seasonal rhythms.
Here is your seasonal breakdown and visitor checklist for the bride of the Mediterranean.

Spring (Mar - May)

Breezy & Blooming

20°C - 25°C | Mild Mediterranean

Lush gardens at Montaza Palace, comfortable seaside walking along the Corniche, and uncrowded historical landmarks.

Summer (Jun - Aug)

Beach & Nightlife

28°C - 31°C | Coastal Breeze

Peak coastal holiday season with buzzing cafes, fresh seafood waterfront dining, lively beaches, and vibrant open-air nightlife.

Autumn (Sep - Nov)

Cultural Season

22°C - 27°C | Ideal Weather

The perfect season for culture enthusiasts. Relaxed visits to the Bibliotheca Alexandrina, Roman Amphitheatre, and Catacombs of Kom El Shoqafa.

Winter (Dec - Feb)

Atmospheric & Moody

14°C - 19°C | Sea Breezes

Experience the famous Mediterranean winter waves (Nawat), dramatic stormy sea photography at Qaitbay Citadel, and historic Greek and Italian cafes.
